# Coca model design

The mature plant is a leafy shrub with a fine woody framework. Its silhouette should come from overlapping oval leaves, not a thick pole and isolated tufts.

## Implementation

All nine production shapes are generated by `python tools/build_coca_models.py`. The generator uses one consistent framework, increasing size and branching over the growth sequence. The mature stage has 12 staggered primary branches, 24 secondary twigs and 123 individual leaves. The leader bends slightly and tapers; branches taper in two connected segments. Leaves alternate along branches and secondary twigs, with both upright and flatter blade orientations. Petioles extend into the opaque part of each leaf texture.

Flowers start in stage 7, with small red fruit in stages 8–9. Both attach to calculated branch positions through short stalks. The existing v4 texture PNGs are retained; crop block texture entries now match the shapes' v4 entries. No crop timing, harvesting, recipes, or drug effects change.

## Review

Run `python tools/preview_coca_models.py` with Pillow and NumPy installed to regenerate the four-angle mature view and nine-stage overview in `docs/previews/`. The preview reads production JSON geometry and PNG textures. It is an orthographic software renderer, not a Vintage Story screenshot; game lighting, ambient occlusion, texture atlas sampling, and wind are not simulated.

JSON, face texture references, UV ranges, transformed bounds and generator reproducibility were checked locally. In-game validation remains required: inspect all stages in daylight, rotate around stage 9, verify foliage visibility and texture resolution, and check selection/harvesting. No Vintage Story runtime is available in this environment.
